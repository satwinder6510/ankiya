FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does “exclusive” actually mean?
Every lead is sold to exactly one agent — you. The traveller's contact details are never shared with another agency on our network. This is what lets you quote on value instead of competing on the lowest price.
How are the leads generated?
We run targeted Meta, Google, and YouTube campaigns in Indian metros and tier-2 cities for specific travel intents. Travellers fill a detailed inquiry form; our team then verifies phone, dates, and budget before routing the lead to a matching agent.
What if a lead is fake or unreachable?
We replace any lead that is a wrong number, duplicate, or clearly not a real inquiry. Just flag it on your dashboard or WhatsApp us within 48 hours and we credit a replacement.
How fast do I get the leads?
Most leads reach you within 2 minutes of submission via WhatsApp. Email and dashboard delivery are also available. For Enterprise plans we offer SLA-backed delivery.
Do you take commission on bookings?
No. You pay only for the lead. Whatever you book, whatever margin you make — it's yours.
Can I pause or cancel?
Yes. Bundles never expire and there are no monthly commitments. Pause whenever you need to, resume when you're ready.
Which cities do you cover?
All major Indian metros plus 60+ tier-2 cities. You can filter leads by traveller's source city so you only receive customers close to your office or service area.
